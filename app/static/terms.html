VILLA28 ENERGY · PERSONAL HOUSEHOLD MONITORING

Terms of use

Effective 15 October 2026. Villa28 Energy is a personal household monitoring application, operated by its owner and intended only for authorised household use.

Monitoring and data quality

The app displays readings supplied by Google Nest, Google Home and Sungrow. Readings may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. Cooling runtime is an estimate from recorded device states, not a measurement of electricity used by an individual AC unit. Solar and grid figures depend on the installation and provider data available.

Permitted use

Use the app only with accounts and devices you are authorised to access. Protect the dashboard password and provider credentials. The app implements no physical thermostat, HVAC or inverter controls; its virtual presence indicator is used to record a household signal.

Availability and support

This is a personal project with no service availability guarantee. It is not an emergency, safety or billing system. For questions, contact the household owner using the support email on the Google consent screen. Google, Sungrow and Railway services remain subject to their own applicable terms.
