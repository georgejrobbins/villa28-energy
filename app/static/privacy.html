VILLA28 ENERGY · PERSONAL HOUSEHOLD MONITORING

Privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026. Villa28 Energy is operated by its household owner for personal home energy monitoring.

Information collected and purpose

After the owner authorises access, the app collects Nest device identifiers and names, temperatures, humidity, setpoints, HVAC activity, modes, reported Eco state, connectivity and event timestamps. It collects available Sungrow installation identifiers, names, solar power and energy, grid import/export and battery readings. Google Home's linked virtual indicator supplies Home/Away transitions and timestamps. These records are used to display household conditions, retain history and help the owner analyse energy use and cooling schedules.

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored to maintain the authorised provider connections. The app does not request Gmail, contacts, camera recordings or precise phone location. Home/Away history can reveal household occupancy and is treated as private household data.

Storage and access

Readings and connection records are stored in the app's PostgreSQL database hosted on Railway. OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted before database storage. Monitoring pages and data APIs require owner authentication. Operational logs record collection and application status; application logging is designed to omit credentials, OAuth codes and raw event payloads.

Sharing and Google data use

The app exchanges authentication and API requests with Google and Sungrow to provide the integrations selected by the owner. Railway provides application and database hosting. Household data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train general-purpose AI models. It is not made publicly available by these information pages.

Villa28 Energy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Historical readings are retained for ongoing household analysis; the application currently has no automatic retention expiry. The owner can revoke Google access through Nest Partner Connections Manager and Google Account permissions, and can revoke Sungrow access through iSolarCloud. Revocation stops future authorised access but does not erase previously stored history. The owner can delete stored records and credentials through database administration; there is currently no self-service deletion button. Hosting backups may retain copies according to the hosting configuration.

Contact and changes

For questions or deletion requests, contact the household owner using the support email displayed on the Google consent screen. This policy will be updated if the application's data practices change.
