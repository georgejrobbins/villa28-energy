Weather or dirty panels?

A lower daily total alone cannot tell us the cause. Cloud, haze, panel temperature, seasonal sunlight, shading, downtime and dirt can all reduce output. We are not yet measuring sunlight or weather alongside this installation, so there is no weather-adjusted performance score.

Next: compare output with sunlight and temperature for the same hours, add the array size/direction/tilt, and record cleaning dates. A persistent shortfall after accounting for weather can flag a problem to investigate; a recovery after cleaning supports dirt as a cause. A few days of readings cannot establish degradation.

How solar performance is assessed · NREL

Individual readings are collected about every five minutes and retained in PostgreSQL for both Nest and solar; they are not replaced by daily summaries. Choose Last 7 days to review a week without averaging away the five-minute samples. History starts when collection began. AC runtime is not AC electricity use; no per-room energy meter is connected.
