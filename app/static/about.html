VILLA28 ENERGY · PERSONAL HOUSEHOLD MONITORING

About Villa28 Energy

Villa28 Energy is a private household application for understanding home comfort and electricity use. It brings Google Nest thermostat readings and Sungrow iSolarCloud solar readings into a password-protected dashboard.

What the application does

With the household owner's permission, the app reads thermostat names, temperatures, humidity, temperature targets, heating or cooling activity, thermostat mode, Eco state and connectivity through Google's Smart Device Management API. It records available solar production, grid and battery information through the official Sungrow developer API.

Readings are normally collected every five minutes. Stored history helps the owner compare cooling activity and solar output over time. A linked Google Home virtual indicator records Home/Away transitions separately; this is not continuous GPS tracking.

Household access

The monitoring dashboard and data exports require the owner's sign-in. This public page contains no household readings. The application does not send commands to thermostats, HVAC equipment or inverters. Its Google Home integration receives commands for a virtual presence indicator only.
